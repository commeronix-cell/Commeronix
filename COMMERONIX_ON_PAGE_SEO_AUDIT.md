## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Overview

On-page SEO forms the direct interface between search engine indexing bots, natural language processing models, and human searchers. In our baseline audit of Commeronix, several systemic on-page deficits were detected:
1. **Truncated or Under-Optimized Title Tags:** Several tools had titles lacking primary keyword modifiers or exceeding Google's 600px desktop SERP display limit.
2. **Missing OpenGraph Metadata:** Over 75% of tool pages and blog articles lacked `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:url`, `og:image`, and `twitter:card`, impairing click-through rates and social graph discovery.
3. **Improper Heading Hierarchies:** Skipped heading levels (e.g., jumping from H1 directly to H3) and missing semantic sectioning existed on multiple utility pages.
4. **Vague Meta Descriptions:** Several meta descriptions lacked active verbs, search intent triggers, and explicit privacy guarantees (such as "100% private, client-side in your browser").

## 3. Heading Hierarchy Verification

Across all 39 HTML documents:
1. **Single H1 Rule:** 100% of pages contain exactly one `<h1>` tag positioned above the main content fold.
2. **Logical Heading Nesting:** No instances of heading skipping (e.g. `H1` followed by `H3` without an intervening `H2`) were detected.
3. **Keyword Density in Subheadings:** Primary and secondary search queries are naturally embedded in `<h2>` and `<h3>` tags without stuffing.

## 4. OpenGraph and Social Graph Audit

Prior to this sprint, social shares of Commeronix URLs fell back to generic homepage previews or broke entirely due to missing tags.

We verified that every single page contains:
- `<meta property="og:type" content="website">` (or `article` for blog posts).
- `<meta property="og:title" content="...">`
- `<meta property="og:description" content="...">`
- `<meta property="og:url" content="...">` (strictly matching the canonical URL).
- `<meta property="og:site_name" content="Commeronix">`
- `<meta name="twitter:card" content="summary_large_image">`
- `<meta name="twitter:title" content="...">`
- `<meta name="twitter:description" content="...">`
