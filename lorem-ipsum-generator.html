The History and Purpose of Lorem Ipsum in Web Layouts

Lorem Ipsum has been the printing and typesetting industry's standard dummy text ever since the 1500s, when an unknown printer took a galley of type and scrambled it to make a type specimen book. Derived from sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of Cicero's 45 BC treatise "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (The Extremes of Good and Evil), it provides a natural distribution of letters that closely mimics English prose.

Using readable English text during early UI mockups often distracts stakeholders who focus on reading the copy rather than assessing visual hierarchy, whitespace, and typography balance. Lorem Ipsum ensures design reviews concentrate on aesthetics, typography, and UX flow.
