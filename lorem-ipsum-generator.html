The History and Purpose of Lorem Ipsum in Modern Web Design

Lorem Ipsum has been the printing and typesetting industry's standard placeholder dummy text for over five centuries. Its enduring utility stems from an essential psychological insight: when people review a visual mockup containing readable English prose, their attention is involuntarily drawn to reading the sentences rather than evaluating typographic scale, visual hierarchy, line height (leading), and layout balance.

Cicero's Classical Treatise: Unpacking the Latin Origins

Contrary to common belief, Lorem Ipsum is not random gibberish. In 1982, Latin scholar Richard McClintock discovered that the text originates from sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of Cicero's 45 BC philosophical dialogue "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (The Extremes of Good and Evil), a discourse on ethical theory popular during the European Renaissance:

  • Original Passage: "Neque porro quisquam est qui dolorem ipsum quia dolor sit amet, consectetur, adipisci velit..."
  • English Translation: "Neither is there anyone who loves, pursues, or desires to obtain pain of itself, because it is pain, but occasionally circumstances occur in which toil and pain can procure him some great pleasure."
  • The Scrambled Modification: During the 16th century, an anonymous printer excised words, introduced intentional typographical variations, and created the standardized galley specimen that digital software still relies on today.
