The Role of Reading Estimates in Reader Retention

Displaying an accurate reading time badge at the top of long-form articles increases page engagement by setting realistic expectations for visitors. Studies show that when readers know an article takes only 3 to 5 minutes to complete, they are 40% more likely to finish the piece rather than bouncing immediately.
