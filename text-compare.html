How Text Diff Algorithms Work in Modern Development

Text comparison engines calculate the Longest Common Subsequence (LCS) between two arrays of lines or tokens. Originally developed by Eugene Myers in 1986, diff algorithms identify the minimal sequence of edits (additions and deletions) required to transform one string into another.

Whether you are inspecting code commits, proofreading contract revisions, or verifying that copy formatting has been preserved during CMS migration, client-side diff comparison keeps your proprietary documents completely private.
