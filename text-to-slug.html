The Science of SEO-Friendly URL Slugs

Google's Search Central documentation explicitly recommends using clean, descriptive words in URL slugs rather than unreadable IDs or dynamic queries. Here are the core technical rules for URL design:

  • Use Hyphens, Not Underscores: Search engines recognize hyphens (-) as word separators. In contrast, underscores (_) are often treated as word joiners.
  • Omit Stop Words: Eliminating words like "a", "an", "the", and "of" makes the URL concise, increasing click-through rates (CTR) on Search Engine Results Pages (SERPs).
  • Keep it Under 60 Characters: Brief URLs are easier to share across social media and improve mobile readability.
